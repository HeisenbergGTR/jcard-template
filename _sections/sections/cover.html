Print Quality

Adjust

On the card: drag the cover to move it and scroll over it to zoom. Click it, then use the arrow keys to nudge, + and − to zoom, [ and ] to rotate, and 0 to reset. Hold Shift for bigger steps.

Cover images are saved with the J-card at full quality. You can also drop an image anywhere on the page, or copy one and press Ctrl+V.

Find Cover Art

Links marked with a magnifier (🔍) search using the card's titles, with the lower title as the artist and the upper title as the album. Save or copy the image you want, then drop or paste it here. Album art is copyrighted, so only print it for your own tapes.

  1. COV (Music Hoarders): searches many stores at once and shows full-size resolutions. 🔍
  2. MusicBrainz / Cover Art Archive: open database with original-resolution scans, often including cassette releases and track lists. 🔍
  3. Discogs: the biggest cassette catalogue, with photos of real J-cards (search filtered to cassettes). 🔍
  4. iTunes Artwork Finder: clean, square Apple Music artwork, often 3000 px or more.
  5. Album Art Exchange: user-submitted high-resolution scans and restorations.
  6. Bandcamp: independent releases; the album page's art opens at full size. 🔍
  7. Last.fm: broad coverage, including obscure releases. 🔍
  8. fanart.tv: artist logos, CD art and backgrounds, which are handy for spines and backs.
  9. Google Images (large): a catch-all, limited to large images. 🔍
  10. Tapedeck.org: a gallery of vintage blank-cassette designs for retro inspiration.
