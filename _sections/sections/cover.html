Adjust

On the card: drag the cover to move it and scroll over it to zoom. Click it, then use the arrow keys to nudge, + and − to zoom, [ and ] to rotate, and 0 to reset. Hold Shift for bigger steps.

Cover images are saved with the J-card at full quality. You can also drop an image anywhere on the page, or copy one and press Ctrl+V.
